Field guide

Make your own course.

Walk
WASD or arrow keys. Hold Space to sprint.
Move earth
Hold left click near your character to shovel. Each scoop builds a pile beside the hole. Right click smooths the ground.
Stones & machines
Walk close and press E to pick up, enter or exit.
Drive
W / S forward and reverse, A / D steer. Dig lowers the bulldozer blade; hold dig for repeated excavator scoops. Z / X rotates the excavator cab.
Look around
Scroll to zoom. Q or [ / ] rotates the view. Overview shows the landscape.
Touchscreen
Drag the left side to walk. Hold Dig or Smooth to work the ground in front of you. Drag the right side to look around. Pick up enters nearby machines. Hold Sprint to run.
